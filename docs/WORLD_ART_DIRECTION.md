# Netbound World Art Direction

Date: 2026-07-29
Scope: three production worlds across thirty levels

## Creative Contract

Netbound is a Trajectory Playground arcade course, not a photoreal stadium sim.
Worlds change atmosphere, props, and presentation accents. They never change
ball physics, scoring geometry rules, star math, wallet rules, or quality-tier
gameplay signatures.

## World Map

| World | Levels | Identity | Atmosphere |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 Training Yard | 01–10 | sunny practice field | cones, padded blockers, rebound boards, fencing |
| 2 Street Arcade | 11–20 | urban court course | barricades, stop-sign spinners, scoreboard gates |
| 3 Stadium Showdown | 21–30 | championship arena | floodlights, flags, seating silhouettes, scoreboards |

## Shared Rules

- Keep goal frames white in every world.
- Keep nets readable and non-blocking.
- Course art wrappers follow collision bodies exactly.
- Decorative geometry has no collision.
- Low quality keeps silhouette identity and drops secondary marks.
- No logos, characters, or copyrighted assets.

## World Palettes

### Training Yard

- Sky: bright mid-day blue
- Field: vivid practice green
- Accents: coral pads, teal rebound faces, yellow motion marks
- Backdrop: simple fencing and practice banners

### Street Arcade

- Sky: warmer urban daylight / late-afternoon court light
- Field: deeper court green with chalk-adjacent lines
- Accents: striped barricades, teal scoreboard panels, coral safety stripes
- Backdrop: painted walls and compact court rails

### Stadium Showdown

- Sky: deeper arena blue with floodlight warmth
- Field: richer match green
- Accents: navy frames, teal panels, restrained gold trim
- Backdrop: seating bands, flags, floodlight masts, scoreboard silhouettes

## Presentation Accents

- Level Select groups markers by world with short section headers.
- Level entrance can show world name + level title briefly.
- Result motifs tint by world without changing success copy hierarchy.
- Reduced Motion keeps titles readable and skips decorative motion.

## Non-Goals

- Physical-device beta prep
- Real ads / billing / analytics
- Cloud saves / accounts
- Level editor / share codes
